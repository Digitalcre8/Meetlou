Questions

How does Lou cut chasing calls?

Clients see what happens next, what they still owe you and a plain-English summary of every call, on their phone, whenever they want. Most chasing calls are a client asking something that's already on the file. When they can see the file, they don't need to ask.

How much time will it save us?

It depends on your firm, which is why we take a four-week baseline before a pilot and measure against it. As an illustration, a practice with four fee earners and sixty matters could get back around 26 hours a month, mostly from attendance notes and fewer status calls. The worked example is on the pricing page.

How does this help if a complaint does land?

You place the matter on legal hold, which pauses retention so nothing can be deleted. Then you export the bundle: every call, transcript, approved summary, email, document, read receipt and the consent log, in date order and timestamped. You answer from a record made at the time instead of reconstructing one from memory.

Will the record stand up as evidence?

Lou is built so the record can be relied on: every recording follows a consent announcement, nothing can be altered within the retention period, and every access and approval is logged. Whether a particular record settles a particular dispute is for your firm and its advisers. What changes is that you have the record at all.

Do clients have to agree to be recorded?

Every call starts with an announcement before anything is recorded, and a caller who would rather not be recorded can hang up and email instead. Your compliance officer approves the exact wording before the first call.

Do fee earners have to change how they work?

Very little. Clients use the matter's line and address, and every email still lands in the fee earner's own inbox. The new habit is reading and approving each summary before the client sees it.

If the other side's firm also uses Lou, can they see our files?

No. Every firm's records are kept apart, and Lou acts for each firm separately. Two firms on the same transaction share nothing.

Does Lou replace our case management system?

No. Your case management system records what the firm files. Lou captures what happens around it: the calls, the client email, the agent chasing. Integrations with LEAP, Actionstep and Osprey ship with the first firm that needs one.

Step 5 · If a complaint lands

One action holds the file. One more builds the bundle.

The compliance officer places the matter on legal hold, which pauses retention so nothing can be deleted. Lou then assembles the bundle: every call, transcript, approved summary, email, document, read receipt and the consent log, in date order.

The rules haven't changed. The firm has eight weeks to give a final response, and that response must tell the client they can take it to the Legal Ombudsman within six months. Lou tracks the clock. It doesn't answer for you.

The rulesEight commitments
1

Consent before every recording

Every caller hears an announcement before anything is recorded. Your compliance officer approves the wording.

2

The firm is the controller

Lou processes on your behalf under a data processing agreement. You own the record and can take all of it with you.

3

Privilege stays with the firm

Recordings and summaries are held for the firm and never shared without your instruction.

4

Retention and erasure

Records can't be altered within your retention period. Deletion happens only in a scheduled, audited run that records the fact of deletion and not the content.

5

Legal hold

When a complaint lands, one action pauses retention on the matter, and the firm exports a timestamped bundle.

6

Scoped to the matter

Each matter has its own line and address, so nothing outside it reaches the platform.

7

The people helping the client

A client can bring in a parent or partner to see their timeline. The firm controls what is shared, and every grant is logged.

8

The other side's firm

If the firm on the other side of the transaction also uses Lou, it sees nothing of yours. Every firm's records are kept apart.

Lou staff access

We can't open your files without asking.

If our support team needs to look at a matter's content, they request it with a reason. Your compliance officer approves or declines. Access is time-limited, and every request, approval and view appears on a log your compliance officer can read at any time.
