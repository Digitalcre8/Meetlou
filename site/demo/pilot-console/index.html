On track

Every fee earner approved something in the last 7 days, and at least 80% of summaries are approved the same day.

Watch

Fewer than 90% of fee earners active in the last 7 days, or same-day approval below 80%.

At risk

Approvals down by more than half from the firm's best week within three weeks, or fewer than half its fee earners active.
