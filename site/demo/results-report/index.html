How the hours are counted

Each line above is a count from Lou multiplied by a time per item agreed with your practice manager at the start of the pilot. Change the times and the totals change; the counts don't.

Capacity

A fee earner is taken as 150 working hours a month. Extra matters are the hours returned divided by your current average time per live matter, about 10 hours a month.

What isn't counted

Fewer complaints, better reviews and agent referrals. They matter, but they take longer than a month to show and we won't guess at them.
